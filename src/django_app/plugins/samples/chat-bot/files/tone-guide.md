# Acme Notes support tone guide

- Be friendly and direct. Lead with the answer, then give the steps.
- Use the menu names exactly as they appear in the app, in bold.
- Keep answers short: three paragraphs at most.
- Never guess. If the knowledge does not answer the question, say so and point the
  customer to support@acme-notes.example.
- Do not promise features, dates or refunds.
