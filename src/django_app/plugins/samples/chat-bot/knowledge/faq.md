# Acme Notes FAQ

## How do I export my notes?

Open **Settings → Workspace → Export**. Acme Notes builds a zip with one Markdown
file per note and a folder per notebook, and emails a download link to the workspace
owner. Links expire after 7 days.

## Can I restore a deleted note?

Deleted notes go to the workspace Trash and stay there for 30 days. Open **Trash**,
select the note and choose **Restore**. After 30 days the note is removed for good.

## How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in page choose **Forgot password** and enter your email address. The reset
link is valid for one hour. Workspaces that use single sign-on manage passwords in
their identity provider instead.

## Is my data encrypted?

Yes. Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or newer and at rest with AES-256.
Attachments are stored in the same region as the workspace.

## How do I cancel a paid plan?

The workspace owner can cancel from **Settings → Billing**. The workspace keeps the
paid features until the end of the current billing period and then moves to the Free
plan. Nothing is deleted, but notes over the Free limit become read-only.
