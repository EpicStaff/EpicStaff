# Acme Notes product overview

Acme Notes is a note-taking app for small teams. Notes are written in Markdown and
organised into notebooks. Every notebook belongs to one workspace, and a workspace
can hold any number of notebooks.

## Plans

- **Free**: one workspace, up to 3 members, 500 notes and 1 GB of attachments.
- **Team**: $6 per member per month, unlimited notes, 50 GB of attachments per
  workspace, version history for 90 days.
- **Business**: $12 per member per month, everything in Team plus single sign-on,
  audit logs and version history with no time limit.

## Apps

Acme Notes runs in the browser and has desktop apps for Windows, macOS and Linux and
mobile apps for iOS and Android. Notes sync between devices within a few seconds.
The desktop and mobile apps keep a full offline copy of every notebook the user has
opened in the last 30 days.

## Sharing

A note can be shared with workspace members (view or edit) or published as a
read-only public link. Public links can be switched off at any time from the note's
Share menu. Workspace owners can disable public links for the whole workspace.
